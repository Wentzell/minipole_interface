About minipole_interface

minipole_interface is a TRIQS-side wrapper around the Green-Phys/MiniPole analytic continuation package. The Minimal Pole Method (MPM) algorithm — the matrix-valued ESPRIT step, the conformal maps, and the DLR / real-frequency variants — is implemented upstream by the Egull group. This package pins the mini_pole==0.7 PyPI release and exposes its functionality through TRIQS Green’s-function containers (Gf / BlockGf on MeshImFreq, MeshDLRImFreq, MeshDLRImTime).

A sister TRIQS-orbit package, adapol, provides a different analytic continuation algorithm (AAA + semi-definite programming). The two coexist; pick whichever matches your data.

Authors

Wrapper layer: N. Wentzell.

Upstream MiniPole authors: L. Zhang, Y. Yu, and E. Gull (University of Michigan).
